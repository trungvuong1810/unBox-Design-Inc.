(About the studio)

We close the gap between beautiful and buildable — so your product leaves the concept phase and arrives on a shelf.

Founded by industrial designer Trung Vuong, unBox is a Toronto consultancy that refuses to let great ideas get stuck as renderings. Every surface we draw is drawn with a tool path, a mould split or a print orientation in mind.

Whether you are manufacturing locally or abroad, we stay with the product past the hand-off: tolerances, materials, assembly, cost. The best designs aren’t just beautiful — they are manufacturable.
