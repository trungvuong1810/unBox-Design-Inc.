Acorn Lamp

A sculptural floor lamp that sits between high art and everyday lighting — and a unibody that challenges how lamps are normally made.

Category
Lighting · Home
Process
Large-format FDM / SLS printing
Material
Diffusion-grade polymer
Software
Rhino, Grasshopper
Versions
Corded · Cordless
Status
Functional prototype
Deliverables
Print files, bill of materials
Seams & joints00

The entire body is printed as one cohesive unit. Nothing to glue, nothing to hide.

(01) Form

Borrowed from the forest floor.

The silhouette is taken from the acorn: a soft, swelling body under a textured cap — an object that feels found rather than manufactured. At floor-lamp scale, that familiar shape becomes something closer to sculpture, a presence in the room even when it is switched off.

When it is switched on, the body becomes the light. There is no separate shade and fitting; the whole form glows.

(02) Unibody

One print. No seams. A lattice doing two jobs.

Traditional lamps are assemblies: shells, shades, brackets and fasteners, each with a parting line to hide. The Acorn Lamp uses advanced additive manufacturing to print the entire body as a single unit, eliminating visible seams and joints altogether.

Inside, a generative lattice — developed in Rhino and Grasshopper — works with the outer shell. It stiffens the thin skin so the form holds its shape at scale, and it breaks up the light source so the glow is even and soft, without hot spots, while keeping the signature Acorn profile.

(a) Corded

For a permanent place

A traditional corded configuration for a reading corner or a living room that the lamp will call home.

(b) Cordless

For wherever the evening goes

A high-performance cordless version for flexible, portable ambience — on a terrace, a table, a shelf.

(c) Ready to make

Print files & BOM

Delivered as production print files and a full bill of materials, so the prototype can become a product.
