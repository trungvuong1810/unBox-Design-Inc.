Work (03)

A small, deliberate body of work. Each project is a different kind of problem — a wearable with no electronics, protective equipment built in a crisis, a lamp that is printed as one piece — and each one had to survive contact with manufacturing.

Disciplines
Industrial design, mechanical design, prototyping, DfM
Sectors
Fitness, medical, lighting & home
Processes
FDM, SLS, large-format printing, moulded components
01 — Wearable

Xamrai Gym Band

A fully mechanical, 3D-printed wristband that counts sets and reps with a click — no battery, no screen, no notifications.

→
02 — Medical

Medical Respirator

A reusable, medical-grade alternative to disposable N95s, with a soft-touch seal and swappable filter media.

03 — Lighting

Acorn Lamp

A sculptural floor lamp printed as a single seamless body, in corded and cordless versions.
