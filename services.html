Services, unpacked

We take products from napkin sketch to market-ready prototype, keeping the design’s integrity intact at every step — and keeping an eye on the factory floor the whole way.

Engagements
Full product development · Contract design
Clients
Founders, start-ups, product companies, makers
Manufacturing
Local or overseas, low or high volume
Based
Toronto, Canada · working remotely worldwide
(01)

Product design

Strategic, end-to-end product development. You bring the idea — or just the problem — and we carry it through research, concept, form, engineering and prototypes to a set of files a manufacturer can quote and build.

Best for: new products, start-ups preparing to launch, companies extending a product line.

  • Research & brief
  • Sketching & concepts
  • Form & CMF
  • 3D CAD
  • Renderings
  • Prototypes
(02)

Contract design

An industrial designer on your team without a full-time hire. We plug into your workflow, your CAD standards and your schedule — for a launch crunch, a specialist sub-assembly, or a project that needs a fresh pair of hands.

Best for: in-house teams at capacity, agencies needing industrial design support, engineering firms needing form.

  • Hourly or project-based
  • Remote or on-site in the GTA
  • CAD clean-up & rebuild
  • Design reviews
(03)

Design for manufacturing

Where good-looking concepts usually go to die — and where we like to work most. We optimise parts, materials and assemblies to reduce production cost and risk without sanding the character off the design.

Best for: designs that are “almost there”, quotes that came back too high, parts that won’t mould or print cleanly.

  • Draft, wall & rib design
  • Part consolidation
  • Tolerance stacks
  • Material selection
  • Assembly simplification
(04)

Prototyping

Ideas get honest in your hands. We build looks-like and works-like prototypes — from quick printed form studies to fully functional assemblies — so decisions are made on real objects, not renderings.

Best for: user testing, investor demos, validating mechanisms and fit before tooling.

  • FDM & SLS printing
  • Large-format prints
  • Mechanism testing
  • Fit & finish models
(05)

Production support

A design isn’t finished when the files are sent. We help you navigate modern production — local or abroad — with supplier-ready documentation, sample reviews and fixes when first articles don’t match the intent.

Best for: first production runs, switching suppliers, scaling a printed product to moulded parts.

  • Production CAD & drawings
  • Bill of materials
  • Supplier communication
  • Sample & first-article review
